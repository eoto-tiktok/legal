Privacy Policy - EOTO Reels Publishing

Last updated: 9 November 2026

EOTO Reels Publishing is a private tool operated by the creator of the Each One Teach One podcast. It is used only to publish the creator's own video reels to the creator's own Facebook Page, Each One Teach One.

What the app does

The app connects to one Facebook Page (the Page managed by the app's owner) through the Facebook Graph API. It uploads video files and captions that the owner prepared and publishes them as reels on that Page, at times the owner chose.

What data we handle

  • An access token for the owner's Facebook Page, stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.
  • Publication status (scheduled, published, error) written to the owner's own spreadsheet.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person, including people who watch or comment on the reels.
  • The app is not offered to the public. No one except the owner logs in to it.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Data sharing

Data is sent only to Meta (Facebook), as required to publish the owner's reels. It is not shared with any third party.

Data retention and deletion

The app can be removed at any time in Facebook settings (Settings and privacy, Settings, Business integrations, remove the app) or by the Page admin. On removal the app loses all access. To ask for deletion of any data, write to the address below.
