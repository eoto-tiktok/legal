Privacy Policy - EOTO TikTok

Last updated: 6 November 2026

EOTO TikTok is a private tool operated by the creator of the Each One Teach One podcast. It is used only to publish the creator's own videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

What the app does

The app connects to one TikTok account (the account of the app's owner) through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. It uploads video files and captions that the owner prepared and publishes them on that account.

What data we handle

  • An access token for the owner's TikTok account, stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's account (name, avatar) returned by TikTok at login.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to publish.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other person.
  • The app is not offered to the public. No one except the owner logs in to it.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Data sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok, as required to publish the owner's videos. It is not shared with any third party.

Data retention and deletion

The access token can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services). On revocation the app loses all access.
